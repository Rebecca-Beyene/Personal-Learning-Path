Privacy Policy

Dayvra · Last updated: May 17, 2026

Dayvra ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Dayvra mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide you with a personalized scheduling experience:

  • Account information: Your email address, used solely for authentication.
  • Schedule preferences: Your goals, focus limits, meal/snack break preferences, and flexibility settings.
  • Fixed obligations: Time blocks you enter such as work hours, family commitments, or recurring events.
  • Schedule activity: Which scheduled blocks you mark as completed or missed, used to improve your future schedule.

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any financial information directly. Payment is handled entirely by Apple.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate and adapt your personalized weekly schedule.
  • To send daily check-in reminders and weekly review notifications (only if you grant permission).
  • To improve your schedule over time based on what you complete and what you miss.
  • To authenticate your account and sync your data across devices.

3. AI and Third-Party Services

Dayvra uses third-party services to deliver its core features:

  • OpenAI API: We send your schedule preferences and obligations (never your name or email) to OpenAI's API to generate schedule suggestions. Per OpenAI's API data policy, data submitted via the API is not used to train their models. Each request is isolated — your data is never shared with or accessible by other users. OpenAI's privacy policy is available at openai.com/privacy.
  • Supabase: Your account and schedule data is stored securely in Supabase with row-level security — meaning your data is mathematically isolated from all other users at the database level. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription status is managed through RevenueCat. We do not store your payment information. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen within the app, which permanently removes all your data from our systems.

5. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with encryption at rest. Database access is protected by row-level security policies that prevent any user from accessing another user's data.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you (available in-app).
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time (Settings → Delete Account).
  • Opt out of notifications at any time (Settings → Daily Reminders, or iOS Settings).
  • If you are in the EU: request data portability or lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

7. Children's Privacy

Dayvra is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Push Notifications

We request permission to send push notifications for daily check-in reminders and weekly schedule reviews. This permission is entirely optional — the app functions fully without it. You can enable or disable notifications at any time in the app Settings or your iPhone Settings.

9. Microphone

We request microphone access solely to allow you to speak your tasks and obligations out loud. Audio is processed on-device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will notify you via email or an in-app notice. Your continued use of Dayvra after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
